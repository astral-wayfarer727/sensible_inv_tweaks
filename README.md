# Full list of Changes

## Loot Tables - Environment

- Nuggets removed from all loot tables, replaced with an appropriate amount of ingots where applicable
- Villager Butcher chests meats replaced with Flank
- Sticks removed from dropping from Leaves
- Amethyst Block needs Silk Touch, shatters into shards with the same rate as Glowstone Dust otherwise
- Moss Blocks, Moss Carpets and their Pale variants require Silk Touch
- Big Dripleaf, Small Dripleaf, Spore Blossom, Kelp, Firefly Bush, Crimson Roots, Nether Sprouts, Warped Roots, Tiwsting and Weeping Vines now need Shears
- Short Grass, Tall Grass, Fern and Large Fern now require a Hoe to be held to drop Seeds
- Gilded Blackstone only drops itself now
- Cracked Bricks now require Silk Touch
- Nether Gold Ore drops Raw Gold with the same rates as the Overworld Gold Ore, texture changed to reflect this
- Beetroot Seeds removed from every loot table bar Archaeology, so they can still be obtained somewhere for Villager Trading
- Abandoned Mineshaft chests now contain Raw Ore instead of Ingots
- Hoglin Stables now contain Raw and Cooked Flank instead of Pork
- Glowing Arrows removed from all Bastion loot tables
- Buried Treasure only contains Crop items
- Removed Spider Eyes from Desert Temples, added a Gold Block as a rare item
- Added a Diamond Block to End Cities as a rare item
- Added an Emerald Block to Jungle Temples as a rare item
- Removed Dark Oak Logs and Tripwire Hooks from Pillager Outposts
- Removed many items from Ruined Portal chests, Lodestones, literally everything golden related, substituted Gold Ingots with Iron
- Removed the Marked Map from Shipwrecks since Buried Treasure is no longer... treasure
- Leather Armour removed from Supply Shipwreck chests
- Diamonds removed from Treasure Shipwreck chests

## Loot Tables - Mobs

- Armadillos and Turtles can drop their Scutes on death with a 50/50 chance, quantity increases with Looting, brushing Armadillos still works
- Made Blaze Rod dropping a bit more forgiving, and made Breeze Rod drop rates match
- Removed the Tipped Arrows from Skeleton Variants
- Chicken and Rabbit now drop Haunch instead of their respective meats
- Cow, Pig (+Hoglin), and Sheep now drop Flank instead of their respective meats
- Warm biome variant animals drop more meat and less hide, and cold biome variant animals do the opposite
- All Chicken now lay standard Eggs, variants can still be bred
- Rabbits drop Leather instead of Rabbit Hide
- Cod and Salmon now drop Fish instead of their respective meats
- Both Guardian types no longer drop an assortment of fish, Elder Guardians no longer drop Sponges and now drop Heart of the Sea, chance increases with Looting
- Endermite are slightly less of an annoyance where they now have a chance to return your Ender Pearl upon death, chance increases with Looting
- Goats now can drop their horns on death, chance inscreases with Looting. However, all goats will only drop the regular Goat Horn, headbutting is the better option
- Nautilus Shell drop chance from Nautilus raised, Zombie Nautilus can also now drop the shell
- Piglins can drop Gold Ingots, Brutes dropping them at a higher chance and can rarely drop Netherite Scrap
- Polar Bears no longer drop anything
- Shulker Shell drop chance from Shulker riased
- Zombie Piglins only drop Rotten Flesh
- Glowing Arrows removed from Piglin Bartering tables

## Recipe Changes

- All Fences now give 12 instead of 3
- All Fence Gates now give 6 instead of 1
- All Trapdoors now give 12 instead of 2
- All Wood now gives 4 instead of 3
- All Rails now give 64 instead of varying amounts
- All Lanterns now give 4 instead of 1
- Ladders now give 9 instead of 3
- Redstone Lamps now give 4 instead of 1
- Redstone Torches now give 4 instead of 1
- Scaffolding now give 9 instead of 6
- Armour Stand uses a full Smooth Stone block instead of a slab
- Grindstone uses a full Stone block instead of a slab
- Chests and Copper Chests now give 4 instead of 1
- Barrels now give 4 instead of 1
- Barrels are now crafted with Planks in a Cauldron shape with a Trapdoor ontop
- Blast Furnace now uses Cobblestone on the bottom
- Bone Blocks now decraft into 4 Bones, and 4 Bones now make Bone Blocks instead of Bonemeal
- Bookshelves and Chiseled Bookshelves now give 3 instead of 1
- Chiseleed Bookshelves no longer need Slabs in the middle row
- Composter are now crafted with Planks in a Cauldron shape
- All Ingots now decraft into 4 Nuggets, and 4 Nuggets now make Ingots instead of 9
- All recipes requiring Nuggets altered to use Ingots
- Dried Ghast now uncraftable
- Packed Mud no longer needs wheat, crafted by 4 Mud blocks in 2x2 form
- Stonecutter now uses Stone Crafting Materials (Cobblestone, Cobbled Deepslate and Blackstone)
- Template duplication only requires 1 Iron Ingot instead of 7 Diamonds

## Recipe Additions

- Powered Rails can now be crafted with Copper Ingots
- Amethyst Blockscan be unpakced into 4 Shards
- Raw Ore Blocks can be Blasted
- Blue Ice can be unpacked into 9 Packed Ice
- Packed Ice can be unpacked into 9 Ice
- Mushroom Blocks can be crafted with 9 of their respective Mushrooms, and can be unpacked back into 9 Mushrooms
- Mushroom Stems can be crafted with 3 Mushrooms in a vertical line
- Brick Blocks can be unpacked into 4 Bricks
- Calcite can be crafted with 2 Quartz and 2 Dripstone Blocks in 2x2 form
- Clay Blocks can be unpacked into 4 Clay Balls
- Cobwebs can be crafted with 2 String, and unpacked back into 2 String
- Horse Armour can now be crafted with the material type in the same formation as Leather Horse Armor, but with Leather Horse Armor in the center grid
- Name Tags can now be crafted with 2 paper and an ingot and gives 4
- Nautilus Armor can be crafted with a Nautilus Shell surrounded by the respective material
- Bricks can be Stonecut from Terracotta
- Dripstone Blocks can now be unpacked into 4 Spikes
- Glowstone can now be unpacked into 4 Dust
- Enchanted Golden Apple can once again be crafted, with 4 Gold Blocks surrounding the Apple and with 4 Experience Bottles in the corners of the grid
- Honeycomb Block can now be unpacked into 4 Honeycomb
- 4 Magrove Roots can now be turned into a Mangrove Log
- Nether Brick Blocks can now be unpacked into 4 Nether Bricks
- Quartz Blocks can now be unpacked into 4 Quartz
- Resin Brick Blocks can now be unpacked into 4 Resin Bricks
- Rooted Dirt can now be crafted from 2 Dirt and either 2 Hanging Roots or 2 Sticks in a 2x2 crafting grid
- Standard Deepslate can now be Stonecut into much more blocks
- Stone can be Stonecut into Cobblestone
- Terracotta can be unpacked into 4 Bricks

## Brand New Additions

- New Lapis Blockset to mirror Quartz
- As mentioned earlier, new meat to group old meat types together
- Breeze Rod Block, crafted from and into 9 Breeze Rods, is a pillar block and can be placed directionally
- Blaze Rod Block, crafted from and into 9 Blaze Rods, is a pillar block and can be placed directionally, and gives out light
